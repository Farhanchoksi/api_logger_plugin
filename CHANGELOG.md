## 1.0.0

* Initial release.
* Features 8 different log styles (`pretty`, `compact`, `minimal`, `curl`, `singleLine`, `box`, `developer`, `emoji`).
* Intercepts and logs HTTP requests, responses, and errors.
* Fully customizable outputs (headers, body, max width).
* Custom request filtering support.
